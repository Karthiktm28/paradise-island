Resort Stay

Paradise Island is an island in the Bahamas, formerly known as Hog Island. With an area of 277 hectares (685 acres), it sits just off the shore of Nassau, on the northern edge of New Providence island.

Best known for the sprawling Atlantis resort — featuring water parks, pools, beaches, restaurants, a walk-in aquarium, and casinos.

Paradise Island is connected to New Providence by two bridges crossing Nassau Harbour. The first was built in 1966; the second in the late 1990s.
